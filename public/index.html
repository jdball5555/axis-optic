Our story

Built by someone who’s worked your shift.

I’m a Chick-fil-A leader, and I built Axis Team for the job I do every day.

I’ve closed after the last car pulled through, covered the call-out ten minutes before the dinner rush and bounced between three different apps in the middle of a shift just to see who’s working and where. Every screen in Axis Team started as a problem I ran into on a real shift.

Axis Optic is a small, founder-led startup. I’m not a software company guessing at what restaurants need. I’ve been in the trenches doing the same work you do, and I’m building the tool I wish I’d had.

DB David Ball
Founder, Axis Optic · Chick-fil-A leader
